How to think like Cialdini

A free AI skill built on Robert Cialdini’s 7 principles of influence. Three steps, one card.

1

Add the skill

Download the skill folder from this repo, zip it and add it to Claude. Free to copy and use.

Settings→Skills→Upload
2

Say the magic words

Start a chat. Say the trigger, then tell it about your campaign, brief or ad.

“Think like Cialdini.” We’re launching a project-management app to small creative agencies.
3

Get your card

One phrase is all you need. Say “Think like Cialdini” any time you want a fresh card for a new campaign, ad or pitch.
Bonus: once it’s added, Claude chimes in with a quick Cialdini tip whenever you talk campaign strategy.

All credit to Robert B. Cialdini · Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion
