3Social proof

Show them people like them already do it

Seen in action

In Cialdini’s hotel study, “most guests reuse their towels” beat the standard eco-message. “Most guests who stayed in this room” did even better. The closer the crowd, the stronger the pull.

Consider

Agencies buy what other agencies use. Lead your landing page with “Used by 1,200 agencies of 5–20 people” and three logos they’ll recognise, before a single feature.

Also up the sleeve

Authority: put a named agency founder’s quote, with their job title, right next to the sign-up button.

“It’s not a trick if it’s true.”
